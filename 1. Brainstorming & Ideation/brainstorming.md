# Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Title
ComicCraft AI Story Creator

## Problem Statement
Creating a complete comic manually requires time for story writing,
panel creation, and image generation.

## Proposed Idea
ComicCraft AI Story Creator generates a story and converts the idea
into five comic panels using AI.

## Target Users
Students, beginners, and comic creators.

## Main Features
- AI story generation
- Five comic panel generation
- Multiple AI provider support
- Comic image saving
- PDF comic generation

## Expected Outcome
A simple web application that converts a user's story idea into
a comic with a story and five illustrated panels.
