# Project Demonstration

## Project Title
ComicCraft AI Story Creator

## Demonstration Overview

The project demonstration shows the complete working flow of
the ComicCraft AI Story Creator application.

## Demonstration Steps

1. Open the ComicCraft AI web application.
2. Enter a comic story idea.
3. Generate the AI story.
4. Generate five comic panels.
5. Save the generated panel images.
6. Generate the final comic PDF.
7. Open the PDF and verify the story and five panels.

## Demonstrated Output

The demonstration includes:

- AI-generated story
- Five comic panels
- Final ComicCraft AI PDF
- Working web application
